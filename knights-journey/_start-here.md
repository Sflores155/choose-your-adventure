
# A Knight's Journey⚔️ - Episode one: Rescuing The Princess 

You are a Knight tasked by king Richard👑 of one of the all powerful lands to save his daughter. The Princess, from a Dragon who has captured her for his own needs.

No one knows why she was taken. It is up to our hero Edward The Knight🛡️ to figure out why she was taken and to stop the Dragon🐉 from hurting her. 

As you begin your journey of finding out Where the Dragon has taken her you will meet many obstacles along the way. They may help you on your journey, or they may end you. Be careful Lone Knight for you are the King's only hope of saving the royal Princess👸 from certain doom. Good luck soldier and stay safe.
